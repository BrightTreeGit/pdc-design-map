How this was made

Heights are the Queensland Government elevation model sampled every 15 m over a 40 × 36 km area. Hollows were filled so water can always find a way down, then each cell drains to its steepest neighbour. Ground at or below half a metre that connects to the ocean is treated as sea and tidal water. Creek names are not in this data: "likely Eudlo Creek" comes from the route passing beside Eudlo. Town positions are approximate. Bright Tree's boundary and catchment come from the 1 m LiDAR on the design map.
