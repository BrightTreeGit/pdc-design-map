How this was made

Heights are the Queensland Government 2022 LiDAR ground model at 1 m, smoothed slightly for the contours. Flow follows the steepest way down on a 2 m grid with dips filled; a creek line starts where about 3 ha drains to it. Some catchments also take water from beyond the map's east or south edge: their exits carry more than their own area, shown as "plus inflow". The map stops at the LiDAR tile edge, about 900 m east of the boundary and 230 m past the eastern edge of the Bright Tree catchment.
